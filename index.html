Slyxes Creator Studio

A local-first desktop application designed for creators to preview video assets, organize library versions, schedule publishing, and review channel metrics.

🎬 Video Library

Preview rendered MP4 outputs, organize version revisions (v1, v2, v3), and manage storage with automatic pruning of outdated files.

📱 Social Accounts

Direct integration with official platforms including TikTok Login Kit and YouTube Data API. Review status and schedule content safely.

🔒 Local & Private

Credentials and tokens are stored locally on your device with Windows DPAPI encryption. No cloud servers, no data brokering.
